Bio

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. My research focuses on the intersection of speech processing, natural language processing, and reinforcement learning. I received my B.S. in Electrical Engineering from National Taiwan University. I have gained extensive research experience at several top labs, including NTU Speech Processing and Machine Learning Lab, NTU Machine Intelligence & Understanding Lab, NTU Robot Learning Lab, and Tsinghua Embodied AI Lab. Currently, I am a research intern at Microsoft Research. My recent work involves speech foundation models, in-context learning for speech, and imitation learning. I have contributed to multiple publications at venues such as INTERSPEECH and ICML.
