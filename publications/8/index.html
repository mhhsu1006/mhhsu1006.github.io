Geometric Alignment without Functional Equivalence: A Layer-wise Analysis of the Speech-Text Modality Gap

Ming-Hao Hsu, Xiaohai Tian, Jun Zhang, Lu Lu, Yuxuan Wang, Zhizheng Wu

NeurIPS 2026

Project Page Paper OpenReview

Overview

We examine why speech LLMs can respond differently to spoken and written versions of the same question. Across four models, we compare representation similarity with how answer preferences develop through the layers. Speech and text can look similar in intermediate layers while producing different answer margins near the output. Attention interventions distinguish changes in answer separation from changes in correctness, and temporal compression recovers a subset of speech-only errors. These results show why geometric similarity alone is insufficient for evaluating whether a model uses speech and text in functionally equivalent ways.
