Is Text All You Need? Text as a Universal Information Bottleneck for Speech LLMs

Ming-Hao Hsu, Yuxuan Hu, Shujie Liu, Jinyu Li, Yan Lu, Zhizheng Wu

NeurIPS 2026

Paper OpenReview

Overview

Convex Gate (C-Gate) connects a speech encoder to a pretrained language model using continuous mixtures of the model's frozen token embeddings. This constrains the speech interface to the vocabulary's convex hull without requiring discrete transcription. A shared, partially adapted decoder supports speech recognition, emotion recognition, and spoken reasoning. We analyze where task information is stored in the bridge sequence and how the decoder accesses it, while also evaluating changes in its text capabilities. The study examines how a fixed text codebook can carry both linguistic content and acoustic information through continuous, speech-dependent representations.
