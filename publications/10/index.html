Entity Binding Failures in Speech LLM Reasoning: Diagnosis and Chain-of-Thought Intervention

Ming-Hao Hsu, Xiaohai Tian, Jun Zhang, Zhizheng Wu

Interspeech 2026

Paper arXiv Code

Overview

Speech-based reasoning errors are concentrated in particular tasks rather than reflecting a uniform loss of reasoning ability. We study failures to keep track of which claims belong to which entities, using two speech LLMs. Entity-Aware Chain-of-Thought (EA-CoT) asks the model to list entities, record their claims, and then reason through the problem. This inference-time intervention improves entity-tracking accuracy, including cases with misrecognized names. Controlled comparisons separate the benefit of explicit binding from the benefit of a larger generation budget, supporting an elicitation-based explanation of the observed gap.
